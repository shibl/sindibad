Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide game-world concept artwork for Sindibad Studios' The Curriculam, a game spanning the entire Syrian school curriculum.
Primary request: a richly designed, explorable Syrian-inspired adventure-game world seen from an elevated three-quarter camera, rendered as polished stylized 3D game environment art. One connected old city and its surrounding landscape, not isolated floating islands. A winding turquoise water channel links pale limestone courtyards, an open mechanical puzzle workshop with a huge brass gear, a blue-tiled observatory, an orchard, a hilltop archaeological ruin, and a distant green valley. Distinct paths, bridges and geometric floor puzzles make the scene immediately legible as a game environment. Buildings have Syrian courtyard architecture, wooden lattice windows, striped stone arches and flat roof terraces. Warm terracotta, pale stone, deep teal shadows, cobalt tiles, green trees, golden late-afternoon sunlight. Materials are tactile and crafted, with readable architectural forms and restrained detail.
Composition: panoramic 2.3:1 landscape, the complete world fills the frame, layered foreground and horizon, strongest architecture in the centre and right, uncluttered darker blue-green trees/water at the left edge so a website title can overlay it. Confident art direction for an adventure game suitable for varied school ages; sophisticated, not babyish. Eye-level horizon remains horizontal.
Constraints: no text, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no interface, no framing, no stock-photo classroom, no books-on-desks still life, no photoreal tourism, no imitation of an existing game's map. This is visual concept art, not a screenshot.
