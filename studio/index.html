– The Sindibad way

Play comes first.

We bring game developers, AI researchers and educators together around one question: what makes the next moment worth playing?

Arabic from day one.

Arabic and English belong at the beginning of the creative process. Language shapes our stories, interfaces and worlds.

Build for real devices.

Our first voyage is designed for low-powered Android phones. Small downloads and thoughtful systems make room for more players.

Keep the voyage offline.

Sindibad & Yasmina works without an internet connection, so a player can keep exploring wherever the day takes them.

Let curiosity lead.

Every island in our first game is a world to master. We use stories, puzzles and discovery to give each next step a purpose.

Find each player’s rhythm.

Our adaptive engine adjusts difficulty and pacing to the player, keeping the challenge within reach while making progress matter.

Test the journey.

Our player simulator explores games before launch. Simulation helps the team examine pacing and difficulty, then learn from real players.

Make ideas stay.

Memory research informs when an idea returns. We look for ways to make revisiting a discovery feel like part of the adventure.

One crew, many horizons.

With our home port in Dubai and a team across five countries, we bring different disciplines and perspectives to the same voyage.
