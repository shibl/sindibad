Our technology

AI built for learning, not for hype

Four in-house technologies sit underneath our games. They are what let a small team deliver games that adapt to each student and keep the balance between fun and learning.

Student gesture ID

A model that learns how each student interacts with the screen, so the app can tell when the device has been handed to someone else. Teachers and parents get progress they can trust.

Adaptive learning engine

Adjusts difficulty and pacing to each student's capacity in real time, keeping them in flow: challenged enough to stay engaged, never so much that they give up.

Student simulator

Simulated learners of every age play our games thousands of times before a real child does, so we can tune the mix of fun and learning early instead of guessing.

Memory research

We build the latest research on learning and memory into every game, scheduling when facts come back so they move from short-term recall into long-term memory.

Our first game

Sindibad & Yasmina: a voyage through the grade 6 curriculum

Sindibad is an Arabic educational game built for children who have a cheap phone and no reliable internet. Students sail between islands with Yasmina and her hoopoe guide, and each island is a subject: letters, numbers, science and more.

  • Full grade 6 demo: 128 lessons across 9 islands
  • Works completely offline once installed
  • About 4.4 MB, built for low-powered Android phones
  • Arabic-first, right-to-left interface

Lesson content is currently being reviewed by teachers. We'll publish classroom results once pilots are complete.
