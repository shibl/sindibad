§ 02 Technology

AI built for learning.

Four in-house systems sit underneath every game we make.

T.01

Student gesture ID

Learns how each student interacts with the screen, so the app can tell when the device has been handed to someone else. Progress teachers and parents can trust.

T.02

Adaptive learning engine

Adjusts difficulty and pacing to each student's capacity, keeping them in flow: challenged, never overwhelmed.

T.03

Student simulator

Simulated learners of every age play our games thousands of times before a real child does, so we can tune the mix of fun and learning.

T.04

Memory research

The latest research on learning and memory decides when each fact comes back, so it moves into long-term memory.

§ 03 Work · Case study

Sindibad & Yasmina

An Arabic educational game for children with a cheap phone and no reliable internet. Students sail between islands with Yasmina and her hoopoe guide, and each island is a subject.

  • CurriculumGrade 6 demo
  • Lessons128 across 9 islands
  • Size~4.4 MB
  • ConnectivityFully offline
  • DevicesLow-powered Android
  • LanguageArabic, RTL

Lesson content is under teacher review. Classroom results will follow the pilots.
