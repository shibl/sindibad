# رحلات سندباد وياسمينة — Sindbad & Yasmina's Journeys

A free, open-source, offline-first educational web app that turns the
Syrian national curriculum (grades 1–12) into an RPG-style adventure.

## What's playable now

**Twelve voyages, one per school grade** («خريطة الرحلات»), grouped by the
brief's four age stages. Voyage 6 is open; the other eleven are locked
until their worlds are built.

**Grade 6** (الصف السادس): pick Sindbad or Yasmina, sail a fog-covered
archipelago guided by Hudhud the hoopoe, and learn through short
lessons — Arabic grammar, fractions/decimals/percent, angles — each
starting with an illustrated mini-lesson; and through the 3D book islands
below.

**The farm island** («مزرعتي السعيدة»), open from the start: a 3D farm
where the grade-6 agriculture book (التربية الزراعية) is played, not
read. Pick a helper (a bee, a cow, a puppy or a kitty), then plough,
plant, care for and harvest potatoes, tomatoes, cotton and sugar beet in
the book's order; feed and milk the Shami cow; make village cheese; open a
beehive. Seven quests, one per book lesson, each worth stars like any
lesson; harvests are sold to the ship for coins that buy decorations.

**The music island** («جزيرة الأنغام»), open from the start: a 3D toy
island where the grade-6 music book (التربية الموسيقية) is played with
real sounds. Eight places along a winding path — a giant grand piano, a
glass pavilion, a drum circle round a fire, a metronome tower with the
book's note-car racing round it, a cloud raining notes onto the grand
staff, a music library, a black-basalt theatre (after Bosra's) and a
harbour of world instruments. Ten quests cover all twenty lessons and the
projects: play the major scale, find the half tones, sharps and flats,
key signatures, rain notes onto the staff, hear a rhythm and tap it back
on the drum, fill measures, tempo terms, the Longa and the fugue, the
monologue and the choir, Guido's note names and the letters A–G, folk
instruments of the world, and tuning eight water glasses by the book's
fractions before playing them. The helper «نغمة» (a purple note) explains
every step; each quest hangs a golden note on the rainbow gate, and ten
notes play the «sky song». Between quests the piano, the darbuka, the
metronome and (once made) the glasses can be played freely.

**The science island** («جزيرة العلوم»), after the numbers island: a 3D
island where the grade-6 science book, semester 1 (العلوم، الفصل الأول),
is experimented with rather than read. Ten places in four districts, one
per unit: the forest of life and the pond (relationships, food chains and
webs, ecosystems, people and the environment); the forces arena with its
giant spring scale, the three friction slopes and the farm yard (forces,
friction, work); the glass body dome and the skin pavilion (excretion,
kidney health, vertebrates, the skin); the builders' ramp, the machines
square with its noria and قلعة الحصن on the hill (simple machines).
Seventeen quests, one per lesson. Every quest teaches first («أتعلّم»): the
book's experiments as pictures you play (pins and a magnet, the spring
scale, two balls racing, folded paper falling, the kidney as a filter,
fingerprint detective, marbles pulling a car up a ramp, a flag rising on a
pulley…), then asks, in the book's own formats (its «أستنتج» sentences,
sorting, matching, true/false with the fix, sums). The helper «شرارة», a
little lab robot, explains every step; each quest lights a crystal in the
lighthouse, and seventeen light its beam.

**The atlas island** («جزيرة الأطلس»), after the science island: a 3D
island where the grade-6 social studies book (الدراسات الاجتماعية، الفصل
الأول) is explored on maps and timelines. Thirteen places in five
districts, one per unit: the globe observatory, the pyramids, the port of
Carthage and the palace of the state (the Arab homeland, Africa's
civilisations, the state); the Nile valley, Fustat and the Horn of Africa;
the family house and the square of institutions; the Libyan oasis and the
heroes' garden; the Atlas mountains and the Andalusian palace.
Twenty-five quests. Every quest teaches first («أتعلّم»): maps drawn from
the world's real shapes (tap a sea, a river, a mountain range, a city or a
country) and timelines (the canals of Suez, the ages of ancient Egypt, the
liberation of the Maghreb…), then asks. The observatory opens **the atlas
globe**: spin the Earth and tap any country; 71 of them (all 22 Arab
countries among them) have a full card with the flag, capital, currency,
language, the meaning of the name and of the flag, resources and a fact;
two games, «أين تقع؟» (find it on the globe) and «علم مَن هذا؟» (whose
flag?). The helper «سهيل», a little star explorer, explains every step;
each quest lights a ray of the travellers' compass.

**The art island** («جزيرة الألوان»), open from the start: the grade-6 art
book («التربية الفنية البصرية والجمالية») as a 3D island of fifteen places
in the book's order (the square of colour circles, Fatna Kabbouri's house,
the street in perspective, Braque's studio, Palmyra, the African village,
the norias of the Orontes, the cardboard workshop, the realist's studio,
the mandala garden, the Damascene house, the house of ideas, the wall of
lines, the crafts market and the artists' gallery). Twenty-five quests,
one per lesson: each teaches with the book's paintings and words, then
plays its «أحاول» with the fingers — mixing paints, colouring parts of a
picture, finding the vanishing point and planting trees from near to far,
folding a paper butterfly, gluing a collage, continuing rhythms of lines
and beads, completing a symmetric mask, pressing sunken and raised marks
into clay, making a work stage by stage with the right tool. Each quest
hangs its painting in the gallery at the harbour. 🖌️ «مرسمي» is a sheet to
draw freely, with mirror and mandala symmetry. The helper is «ريشة», a
little paintbrush.

**The English island** («جزيرة Hello»), after the numbers island: the
grade-6 English book (Pupil's Book and Activity Book) as a seaside town of
twelve places. Twenty-four quests: the twenty units and the four
revisions, each teaching its words and grammar box first, then playing the
book's exercises (listen and pick, build a sentence, spell, verb forms,
memory, odd one out, adjective order, conversations). 📖 the word book
keeps every word met, with flash cards. A finished quest lights a star
round the HELLO sign.

**On a phone** the game answers the finger: a light tick when a button,
card or spot is touched, a double pulse for a right answer, a buzz for a
wrong one, a drum roll for stars (⚙️ «الاهتزاز» turns vibration off); a
soft ring where the finger touched; press and hold an answer, a card or an
island on the map to hear it read aloud or learn what is there; sort cards
can be dragged into their bins; the phone's back button goes back in the
game; the sea ripples where it is tapped.

All numbers in the game are written with the digits 1 2 3 (owner decision
2026-10-12).
Lessons are played in different ways: multiple choice, sorting, sailing
a number line, steering the ship by an angle, paying at the market,
filling a waterwheel's buckets, and building sentences from word blocks.
Stars lift the fog, islands award badges, finished topics come back for
spaced review, and the treasure island ends with a certificate. Works
fully offline after the first visit; progress stays on the device.

## Running locally

No build step. Serve the repo root over http and open it in a browser:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then open http://localhost:8000/  (it forwards to the game in Web/)
```

(Opening `Web/index.html` directly from disk won't work: the app uses ES
modules and a service worker, which both need http(s) or localhost.)

Handy URL switches: `?time=day|sunset|night` (sky), `?lite=1|0` (force
low-end mode on/off).

## Tests

```sh
cd Web
npm install   # once — installs Playwright and TypeScript for the tests
npm test      # type-check + .js up to date + offline cache-list check + end-to-end play-through
```

The tools and tests need Node 22.18 or newer: some are written in
TypeScript, which Node runs as it is. `npm run typecheck` checks their
types (`Web/tsconfig.json`) and the game's (`Web/tsconfig.game.json`).

The game's code is TypeScript (the app shell, the engines, the islands'
games); the lesson content, the 3D models and the SVG pictures stay plain
JavaScript, and `tests/check-content.ts` checks the content. The browser
still loads plain JavaScript, so playing or previewing needs no build step:
for a game file written in TypeScript (`app.ts`, `core/learn/steps.ts`…),
the `.js` next to it is made from it by `npm run build`, and both are
committed. Edit the `.ts`, then run `npm run build`; never edit that `.js`
(the test fails if it no longer matches).

After adding or changing any file, run `node tools/update-shell.ts` (in
`Web/`) so the service worker caches it for offline play (the test fails
otherwise).

## Publishing

- Push to `staging` → tests → the staging copy at
  <https://shibl.github.io/sindibad/staging/> (marked «نسخة تجريبية»,
  with its own saves, and every island, gate and farm task open).
- Merge into `main` → tests → the live game.

Nothing is published when tests fail. Each publish shows under
Deployments on GitHub (`github-pages` = live, `staging`). Details: step 5 of
[`WORKFLOW.md`](./WORKFLOW.md) and `.github/workflows/site.yml`.

## Layout

- `Web/` — the game (a web app that also installs to the home screen).
  Everything below lives in it.
- `Android/`, `iOS/` — reserved for future native apps; not started.
- `docs/` — the original brief (PDF and Markdown) and screenshots.
- `index.html`, `service-worker.js` (top level) — only forward the live
  site to `Web/` and clear old offline copies from before the move.

Inside `Web/`:

- `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.ts` — app shell: screens, backdrop, boot.
  `app.ts` lists the 3D islands in one table (`ISLANDS_3D`: each one's file,
  map region and music); a new 3D island is one line there.
- `about.html` — the project brief as a long-form page for developers and
  founders (self-contained; light/dark; IBM Plex Sans Arabic with system
  fallback offline).
- `core/` — the engine: map, lesson runner, topic registry, save data,
  sounds, journey page, finale. Never changes when content is added.
  `save.ts` keeps each child's game in the browser; its `GameSave` type
  lists everything a save holds.
  `topics.ts` is the contract with the topic plugins (`TopicDef`, the
  `LessonCtx` a topic gets, its `Result`); `lesson.ts` runs a topic on the
  lesson screen.
- `worlds/chapters.js` — the twelve voyages (one per grade) and the four
  age stages; `core/chapters.ts` draws the voyages chart.
- `worlds/grade6.js` — the grade-6 islands, positions, unlock rules, badges.
- `core/farm/` — the 3D farm island (`farm.ts` game, `scene.ts` camera and
  light, `models.js` every model, made in code); its content (crops, steps,
  helpers, quests, orders) is `worlds/grade6/farm.js`, its quick reviews
  `plugins/agriculture/`.
- `core/sea/` — the 3D sea map, a preview on the staging copy (`sea.ts` the
  sea, ship, fog and camera; `models.js` the islands' landmarks, made in
  code). `core/map.ts` picks it or the flat map; both share the island labels.
- `core/learn/` — what every 3D island quest is made of: `explore.ts`,
  «أتعلّم», the teaching step every quest opens with (a picture with glowing
  spots; each tap opens one idea; the questions come when every spot is
  found), and `steps.ts`, the questions any island can use (choice, the
  book's «أستنتج» sentences, sort, order, match, true/false, a sum, tap the
  part). Islands give them their own pictures. `steps.ts` also holds what
  every island's own games share: the step types, `tracker()` (a miss: the
  hint, then the answer glows), `finish()` («تابِع») and `makeRunStep()` (an
  island's step runner); `util.ts` the small helpers (`shuffle`, `bump`…);
  `svg.ts` the helpers the islands draw their lesson pictures with (`svg`,
  `spot`, `lab`…).
- `core/isle/` — the engine every 3D book island shares (`island.ts`: the
  stage, camera, places, plinths, the quest panel, rewards; `ground.ts` the
  terraces and the path; `kit.ts` small shared models and
  `canvasTextures()`, pictures drawn once as textures). An island is a
  config of it, checked against its `IslandConfig` type; the lessons it
  plays are typed in `types/content.d.ts` (a quest, a station, a step).
  `tests/check-content.ts` checks every island's lessons in a second
  (teaching first, hints, pictures, reviews), as part of `npm test`.
- `core/lab/` — the 3D science island (`lab.ts` its config, `games.ts` its
  steps, `views.js` and `views-machines.js` the book's experiments drawn in
  SVG, `models.js` every model, made in code); its content (stations,
  seventeen quests) is `worlds/grade6/lab.js` and `worlds/grade6/lab/` (one
  file per unit), its quick reviews `plugins/science/reviews.js`.
- `core/atlas/` — the 3D atlas island (`atlas.ts` its config, `games.ts` its
  steps, `views.js` the maps and timelines, `globe.js` the atlas globe,
  `models.js` every model); its content is `worlds/grade6/atlas.js` and
  `worlds/grade6/atlas/` (one file per unit), the countries
  `worlds/atlas/countries.js` (cards and flags) and `worlds/atlas/shapes.js`
  (the world's shapes, from Natural Earth, made by `tools/make-world.mjs`),
  its quick reviews `plugins/social/reviews.js`.
- `core/studio/` — the 3D art island (`studio.ts` its config, `games.ts`
  its hands-on games, `views.js` (with `views-1.js`…`views-3.js`) the
  book's pictures in SVG, `art.js` the little paintings, `easel.ts` the free
  easel, `models.js` every model); its content is `worlds/grade6/studio.js`
  and `worlds/grade6/studio/` (part1–3), its quick reviews
  `plugins/art/reviews.js`.
- `core/english/` — the 3D English island (`english.ts` its config,
  `games.ts` its games, `words.ts` the word book, `views.js` pictures,
  `models.js` every model); its content is `worlds/grade6/english.js` and
  `worlds/grade6/english/` (block1–4), its quick reviews
  `plugins/english/reviews.js`.
- `core/haptics.ts`, `core/touch.ts` — vibrations and how the game answers
  the finger on a phone.
- `core/tunes/` — the 3D music island (`tunes.ts` game, `games.ts` the
  little games each quest step is played with, `synth.ts` the instruments,
  made with Web Audio, `models.js` every model, made in code); its content
  (stations, quests, steps) is `worlds/grade6/tunes.js`, its quick reviews
  `plugins/music/`. It reuses the farm's camera and light (`core/farm/scene.ts`).
- `vendor/three/` — three.js r170 (MIT), kept in the game so the farm, the
  music island and the 3D sea work offline. Loaded only when a child sails
  to one of them (or, on staging, when the 3D sea map is on).
- `plugins/` — topics and reusable game engines. **Adding a lesson:** see
  [`Web/plugins/README.md`](./Web/plugins/README.md).
- `art/art.js` — all art as inline SVG (heroes, Hudhud, dhow, islands…).
- `fonts/` — Baloo Bhaijaan 2 (SIL OFL), bundled for offline use.
- `manifest.json`, `service-worker.js`, `icons/` — PWA / offline support.
- `tools/`, `tests/` — maintainer scripts and tests (not shipped logic),
  moving to TypeScript (`tsconfig.json`). `tools/build.ts` makes the game's
  `.js` files from their TypeScript (`tsconfig.game.json`, `types/`).
